The Fund Desk · First in a Series

A NEW FUND ONBOARDS IN A DAY

Fund II’s first close lands in November — on rails built this fall.

The slowest part of running a fund was never the investing; it was the paperwork between a handshake and a wire. The Operating System collapses it into a single rail: an LP receives an invitation, completes the accreditation questionnaire, signs the documents, is verified, countersigned, and funded — each step stamped, dated, and visible from the fund desk. Capital calls run on the same ledger, and side-letter obligations surface themselves: the two co-invest offers owed each year appear on the docket before they come due, not after. Compliance moves from “TBD” to built-in.

The timing is deliberate. Fund II’s first close is set for November, and an incoming corporate fund partnership — up to $250K per deal, roughly 10 deals over 12 months — will board on the same rail as any other investor. The back office replaces $22–55K a year in fund-administration tooling; the machinery is the story, and the close is the deadline.

The onboarding rail — the fund desk watches every stamp.

The Events Desk

GOLF PARTY RUNS ITSELF

This fall’s Invitational Golf Party will be managed end to end on the Operating System: RSVP to approvals to check-in, foursomes drawn, a live scorecard posted, sponsor deliverables tracked to the letter. The spring Invitational already ran its poker tally on the same machine; the fall outing simply hands it the whole tournament.

The sponsor book is set — “$20,000 title slot, three $8,000 partner slots — deliverables tracked to the letter” — every promise itemized, every item checked off as it ships. The moment a date is picked, RSVPs open the same day.

Situations Wanted

THE TALENT WIRE

Hiring in CPG runs on introductions, and introductions run on timing. The Talent Wire puts both on rails: founders swipe through a wire of operators, and a single swipe starts the intro. It only takes one.

The launch class is 500 vetted operators — brand builders, supply-chain hands, growth leads — selected the way the paper selects everything else: curated, not scraped. Matches land as introductions, in writing, the way good hires have always started.
